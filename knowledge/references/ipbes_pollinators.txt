Title: IPBES Thematic Assessment on Pollinators, Pollination and Food Production (2016), summary notes
Pollinator decline is driven by a combination of habitat loss, pesticide exposure, disease,
and climate stress, with agrochemical load - especially broad-spectrum insecticide use -
identified as a major independent driver. Reducing blanket pesticide application in favor
of threshold-based integrated pest management (IPM) has been associated with measurable
recovery of beneficial insect populations within one to two growing seasons.
