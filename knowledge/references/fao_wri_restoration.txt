Title: FAO/WRI - The Road to Restoration (2019), summary notes
Assisted natural regeneration (ANR), which protects existing root stock and seed banks
from disturbance rather than relying solely on active replanting, is frequently a faster
and lower-cost route to canopy and habitat recovery on degraded land. Riparian buffer
restoration of at least 10 meters width is highlighted as a high-leverage intervention
for simultaneously improving water quality and terrestrial-aquatic habitat connectivity.
