Title: IPCC AR6 Working Group 3 - AFOLU Chapter, summary notes
Agriculture, Forestry and Other Land Use (AFOLU) interventions such as agroforestry,
reduced tillage, and improved grazing management are identified as having substantial
mitigation and adaptation co-benefits. Agroforestry systems in semi-arid and drought-prone
regions improve microclimate buffering and water-use efficiency, while also increasing
on-farm structural and species diversity relative to monoculture baselines. Rest-rotation
grazing systems allow native perennial vegetation to recover, improving both carbon storage
and forage resilience.
